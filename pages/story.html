Building a digital bridge for prosthetic users

How and why I built Porotezsaz — a small online platform that closed the gap between clinical care and the realities amputees face once they walk out of the clinic.

● Founder & Director 2021 → 2025 Tehran, Iran Health-tech · Tele-consultation · SEO

While working in a clinic as an orthotist and prosthetist, I witnessed a recurring challenge that deeply stayed with me. Many amputees, after receiving their prosthetic limbs, were eager to return to daily life — but they often felt unprepared for the realities they would face outside the clinic.

There was a clear gap between clinical care and real-world experience. Patients had questions, concerns, and uncertainties that extended far beyond their appointments, yet they lacked an accessible, reliable source of support. This observation inspired me to take action.

A place built on trust and accessibility

I created a small-scale online platform designed specifically for prosthetic users — a place where amputees could seek guidance, ask questions, and feel supported in their day-to-day lives. Through this website, I provided personalised consultations, addressing their concerns about using prosthetic limbs in real-life situations.

The platform was built on two simple commitments:

  • Users could reach out with their questions and receive expert guidance, directly from a clinician who understood their devices.
  • If technical issues arose, I connected them with a prosthetist colleague for specialised support.

While consultations themselves were free, I received a small commission from technical referrals — which helped sustain the platform and its maintenance without putting a price on a patient's first question.

Stepping away

The experience was incredibly rewarding. Eventually, I relocated to the UK to pursue my PhD, and I made the difficult decision to step away from the platform. Because of its strong visibility and reputation — achieved through effective SEO — it had reached a high ranking in Google search results and was easily accessible to its target audience. This made it valuable enough to sell, ensuring its continued impact.

Why SEO mattered

A key element of this success was my use of SEO skills. I intentionally applied digital strategies to connect healthcare expertise with those who needed it most. I believe strongly that in today's digital era, technology and the internet give us powerful tools to reach and support specific communities — and I am passionate about using those tools to create meaningful, positive impact.
